The platform

Built for conversations
across borders.

LinguaSpire combines familiar video meeting controls with multilingual speech and messaging infrastructure. Some features are experimental and require further validation.

▣

Video meetings

Room creation, invitations, waiting rooms, participant controls and screen sharing powered by LiveKit.

≋

Translated captions (beta)

Speech recognition and translation are integrated for multilingual subtitles. Real-world accuracy and reliability need improvement.

✉

Translated chat (beta)

Messages can be displayed in a participant’s chosen language, with translation service integration.

⌘

Device controls

Microphone, camera, room management, and optional phone-camera pairing workflows.

▦

Meeting scheduling

Plan upcoming sessions and access meeting controls from one dashboard.
